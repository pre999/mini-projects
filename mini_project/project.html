Rock,Paper and Scissor

Rock-Paper-Scissors (RPS) is a simple and classic hand game played between two players. It is often used to make decisions or settle disputes in a quick and fair manner. The game is easy to learn, making it popular among people of all ages.

TIC TAC TOE

Tic-Tac-Toe, also known as Noughts and Crosses or Xs and Os, is a simple yet strategic game played between two players. It is one of the most popular pen-and-paper games, often used to entertain or teach basic logic and strategy.

CURRENCY CONVERTER

A currency converter is a tool or system used to calculate the exchange value of one currency into another. It plays a crucial role in international trade, travel, and financial transactions by helping users understand the value of money across different countries.
